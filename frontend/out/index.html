What is NailRanks

The first honest ranking for nail art.

NailRanks is a global platform where nail artists post their designs and the public rates every one out of ten. No paid boosts, no follower advantage — just real scores from real people, rolled up into local, national, and global rankings.

Here's how the rankings work

01

Artists post their work

Verified nail artists share their designs — with the technique, shape, finish, and brands used.

02

The public rates every post 1–10

Anyone can score a design. No paid boosts, no follower advantage — just honest reactions from real people.

03

Rankings emerge in real time

Average ratings roll up into local, national, and global leaderboards. The best work rises to the top.

04

Themed competitions decide champions

Monthly themed contests let artists compete head-to-head. The community votes. Winners get prizes and permanent recognition.
